Picture a wide green hill on a bright spring morning. At the top of the hill stands a girl in a yellow raincoat, even though the sky is perfectly clear. In her hands she holds a large red kite shaped like a diamond, with a long tail made of three white ribbons.

She starts to run down the hill. The kite lifts, dips once toward the grass, then catches the wind and climbs fast. Watch it rise past a single oak tree, past two crows that scatter, up into the blue.

At the bottom of the hill, an old man on a wooden bench looks up from his newspaper. He shields his eyes with one hand and smiles. His small white dog barks twice at the kite.

The girl stops running. She stands still, the string humming in her hands, and the red kite hangs high above the oak tree like a tiny door into the sky.
